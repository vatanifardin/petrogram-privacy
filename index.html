PetroGram Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026
Developer: Fardin Vatani, Baku, Azerbaijan

1. Summary

PetroGram is a reference app for the oil and gas industry: a unit converter, a glossary, company structures, interview preparation, market prices, personal notes and downstream (refining) explanations. It does not collect personal data, has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and does not share data with anyone. The only data it downloads from the internet is public price data for the Market page.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following in your phone's local storage:

  • your favorite glossary terms, your chosen language and your light or dark theme;
  • the notes you write on the My Notes page;
  • the last prices downloaded on the Market page, so they can be shown without an internet connection.

This information stays on your device and is never sent to us. We cannot see your notes. It is removed when you delete it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include this data in your own Google account backup; we have no access to that backup.

3. Internet use

When you open the Market page, the app downloads public price data from the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) at api.eia.gov. The request contains only the list of prices requested and the app's access key. It contains no personal data and nothing you have typed. As with any internet connection, the EIA's servers receive your device's IP address; the EIA handles it under its own privacy policy, published at eia.gov. The other pages work without an internet connection.

If you tap Contact, Report a problem, Call or Privacy policy, the app hands over to another app on your phone (email, phone or browser). Those apps have their own privacy practices.

4. Emails you send us

If you send us a report or a message, we receive what you send: your email address, your message, any screenshot you attach, and the app version, language and screen name that the email template contains. We use this only to reply to you and to fix problems. We do not sell it or share it with third parties, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

5. Third-party services

The only third-party service the app uses is the EIA's public data service described in section 3. The app contains no advertising or analytics. If these are added in a future version, this policy and the Google Play data safety information will be updated before that version is released.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal data from anyone.

7. Your rights

We hold no personal data about you from the app, so there is nothing to access or delete on our side. You control the data on your device and can delete it at any time. You can ask us to delete any emails you have sent us by contacting us.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.

9. Contact

Приложение сохраняет в локальной памяти телефона:

Запрос содержит только список запрашиваемых цен и ключ доступа приложения — никаких персональных данных и ничего из того, что вы ввели.
